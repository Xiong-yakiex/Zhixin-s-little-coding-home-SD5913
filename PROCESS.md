# Process

## What I Tried

I used GPT to help me find academic papers because I did not know what papers were relevant to my topic, and I was not familiar with APA referencing. I also searched for information about programming skills that could be useful for art and design students.

I chose to approach the topic from the perspective of a designer because my experience of struggling with programming in Unity was real. When I studied Unity, I often copied code from AI or online sources without fully understanding how it worked.

## What I Kept

I decided to keep the ideas of computational thinking, debugging, basic programming knowledge, and problem-solving. These ideas were useful because they connected academic research with my own experience.

I especially kept the idea that learning programming is not only about memorising code. I think learning how to identify a problem, search for information, test a solution, and understand why it works is more important for me as a designer.

I also kept my personal Unity experience because it explains why I chose this topic.

## What I Rejected

I rejected some information about advanced programming and professional software engineering because my project is about designers learning programming, not becoming professional programmers.

I also rejected some of the text generated by GPT because it was too formal and did not sound like my own reflection. I shortened the writing and kept the ideas that were most relevant to my experience.

For the AI-generated art video, I did not try to explain technical ideas that I was not sure I understood. Instead, I kept my personal observation that I found the disappearing and reappearing pixels interesting.

## Problems I Encountered

I had difficulty using GitHub. I used `git push`, but my files did not appear online. I did not understand why, so I eventually uploaded the files manually through the GitHub website.

A programmer friend also helped guide me when I encountered technical problems. This experience made me realise that knowing where and how to ask for help is also an important part of learning programming.
