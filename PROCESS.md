# Process

## What I Tried

I used GPT to help me find academic papers and with my writing because I was not sure which papers were relevant to my topic and I was not familiar with APA referencing. I also searched for information about programming skills that could be useful for art and design students.

I watched an AI-generated art video and was especially struck by the moment when pixels disappeared and then reappeared; I kept this as a personal observation rather than attempting to explain technical ideas I did not fully understand. I found the process visually fascinating and it made me curious about how programming and computation influence digital art.

I had difficulty using GitHub: I used `git push` but my files did not appear online, so I eventually uploaded the files manually through the GitHub website. A programmer friend also helped guide me when I encountered technical problems, and I am grateful for that help.

## What I Kept

I decided to keep the ideas of computational thinking, debugging, basic programming knowledge, and problem-solving because they connected academic research with my own experience. I especially kept the idea that learning programming is not only about memorising code: identifying a problem, searching for information, testing solutions, and understanding why they work is more important for me as a designer.

I also kept my personal Unity experience because it explains why I chose this topic.

## What I Rejected

I rejected information about advanced programming and professional software engineering because this project is about designers learning programming, not becoming professional programmers. I also rejected some of the text generated by GPT that felt too formal or not like my own reflection; I shortened those parts and kept the ideas most relevant to my experience.

## Problems I Encountered

I had difficulty using GitHub (see above). Knowing where and how to ask for help proved important; the assistance from a programmer friend helped me resolve issues and learn where to look for answers.
